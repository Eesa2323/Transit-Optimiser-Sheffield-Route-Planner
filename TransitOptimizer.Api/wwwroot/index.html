Sheffield Route Planner

Compare graph algorithms and watch their data structures work, step by step.

Welcome to the Sheffield Route Planner

This app finds the quickest way between two places in Sheffield using trams and walking links. It also lets you watch how the computer works out the answer, one step at a time.

1. Plan a route

  • Pick a From and a To station on the left (or at the top on a phone).
  • Choose an Algorithm. Each one is a different method of searching.
  • Press Plan route & trace. Your route is drawn on the map, with the total time, each leg, and where to change lines.
  • Swap stations flips your journey around.

2. The four algorithms

  • Dijkstra always explores the closest unvisited station next. It is guaranteed to find the fastest route.
  • A* works like Dijkstra but also uses the straight-line distance to the destination, so it usually checks fewer stations.
  • Breadth-first search finds the route with the fewest stops, even if it is not the quickest in minutes.
  • Bellman-Ford keeps re-checking every connection until nothing can be improved. It is slower but very thorough.

3. The tabs under the map

  • Step-through trace: press Play to watch the search unfold, or use the arrows and the slider to move one step at a time. On the map, green stations are finished, yellow ones are waiting their turn, and the red one is being looked at right now. Next to it you can see the queue of waiting stations and a table of the best times found so far. Click any line in the log to jump to that moment.
  • Compare algorithms: runs all four on your journey and shows how much work each did. The green row has the fastest time. Click a row to trace that algorithm.
  • Graph & data structures: shows how the network is stored (an adjacency list: each station and the stations it connects to) and explains each algorithm and how fast it is.
  • Spanning tree (Kruskal): draws the cheapest set of links that still connects every station, in red on the map.

Good to know

  • The times and station positions are approximate and for demonstration only. This is not a live timetable.
  • You can reopen this guide any time with How does this work?.
